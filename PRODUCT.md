# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FanScale serves Mozambican fans who discover and support creators, creators who publish and monetize content, and internal administrators who operate and moderate the platform. The primary product context is a responsive web application used across phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

## Product Purpose

FanScale connects Mozambican creators with their audiences through discovery, profiles, posts, subscriptions, tips, messages, live experiences, and creator operations. Product success means these workflows remain clear, trustworthy, and usable across supported devices while preserving the existing prototype behavior until production services replace it.

## Brand Personality

Warm, energetic, and locally grounded. The product should feel welcoming and creator-led while retaining the established pink, rose, and stone visual identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid cold institutional fintech styling, generic social-network cloning, excessive decorative effects, and architecture-driven visual changes that erase FanScale's established identity. Do not introduce generic AI-generated visual patterns or a new palette without explicit product direction.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve recognizable FanScale identity while improving structure and usability.
2. Keep creator and fan journeys direct, legible, and mobile-first.
3. Make navigation predictable through stable URLs, clear active states, and graceful recovery.
4. Treat financial, identity, administrative, and role-aware UI as prototype presentation only until backed by server authority.
5. Prefer incremental, reusable foundations over broad visual or state-management rewrites.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for production-facing interaction patterns. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion behavior, accessible dialog lifecycle, responsive reflow, and assistive-technology announcements. Use Portuguese product copy and keep layouts resilient to longer localized content.
